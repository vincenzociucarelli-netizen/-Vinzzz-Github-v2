**original_problem_statement**: The user wants to create a mobile app for Android and iOS for a sports club.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS**:
1.  **User Roles**: The app should support three distinct roles within a single application:
    *   **Admin**: Manages the sports club, creates teams, sets fees, and oversees all data.
    *   **Coach**: Takes attendance by scanning athletes' QR codes.
    *   **Parent/Athlete**: Manages the athlete's profile. For minors, a parent/guardian manages the account.

2.  **Athlete Management**:
    *   Admins can view and manage all athletes.
    *   Parents can register new athletes, providing detailed personal information (name, surname, DOB, fiscal code, residence, contact numbers for athlete, father, and mother) and fiscal data for tax deductions.
    *   Each athlete is assigned a unique QR code upon registration, generated by the app.
    *   An XLS export of all athlete registration data is required.

3.  **Attendance Tracking**:
    *   Coaches scan an athlete's QR code to log their attendance for training sessions.
    *   The system must block athletes from being marked as present if they have an expired medical certificate or unpaid fees. The reason for the block should be displayed.

4.  **Medical Certificate**:
    *   Parents upload a PDF of the athlete's medical certificate.
    *   The upload functionality must work reliably.
    *   The issue date and validity period (in months) of the certificate must be recorded.

5.  **Payments & Fees**:
    *   Each team has a custom annual fee, broken down into 3 installments with specific amounts and due dates.
    *   The first installment is due after a 7-day trial period.
    *   Admins define these fee structures when creating/editing a team.
    *   When an athlete joins a team, they automatically inherit that team's payment schedule.
    *   The app will initially show bank transfer details and a PayPal option for payments.

6.  **Team Management**:
    *   Admins can create and edit teams.
    *   Team details must include a flexible training schedule (day, time, location) and links to championship calendars. The UI for this should be a table-like structure allowing multiple training days.

7.  **UI/UX**:
    *   All date formats must be .
    *   All user text inputs should be automatically converted to uppercase.
    *   Buttons and interactive elements must be placed thoughtfully, avoiding being obscured by native phone UI controls.
    *   Parents should be able to view and edit the data they entered during registration.
